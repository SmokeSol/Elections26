## Core rule

Do not make the voter “more Moroccan” by adding prose, stereotypes or culturally plausible mechanisms. Make the synthetic voter more human by adding **measured dimensions with provenance and uncertainty**.

The V9 catalogue intentionally contains more dimensions than the current inputs can fill. Missing dimensions stay `UNKNOWN`. This is a feature: it exposes what empirical substance is still absent.

## Direct-evidence-only mechanisms

The following may never be generated from age, sex, income, education, urban/rural location, language, religion, territory averages or generic Moroccan priors:

- party affinity or rejection toward a named party;
- personal familiarity, relationship or valence toward a candidate;
- family, neighborhood or workplace vote recommendation;
- community/tribal-party alignment;
- clientelistic exchange or vote buying;
- coercion or pressure;
- a specific campaign contact.

They remain `UNKNOWN` unless explicitly observed upstream.
